Title 
Same Chaos Daemons list with a Knight Tyrant went 3-0, then 4-1 at a 32-player GT: 7-1 over two events
Body 
I run a small Chaos Daemons channel. This week's video follows up on J.R. Cook's "Shadow scrim" list from last episode: Chaos Daemons with an allied Knight Tyrant, which went 3-0 and took 2nd at Halloween Nemesis Monthly (Alberta, 18 players). He took the exact same list to a 32-player, 5-round GT the next weekend and went 4-1 for 3rd. That's 7-1 over two events. Written version below.

The list (Shadow Legion + Warptide, Purge the Foe, 2,000 pts — unchanged from Halloween)
- Bloodthirster (Warlord) with Fade to Darkness: 350
- Poxbringer with Malice Made Manifest: 85 + 10 Plaguebearers: 115
- Sloppity Bilepiper with Leaping Shadows: 80 + 10 Plaguebearers: 115
- Fateskimmer: 95 + 6 Screamers: 160
- 6 Screamers: 160
- 6 Screamers: 160
- Burning Chariot: 115
- 3 Nurglings: 45
- 3 Nurglings: 45
- Allied: Knight Tyrant 390, Chaos Lord with Jump Pack 85

Nemesis Fall 2026 - The Movie - The Game (Alberta, Nov 7-8, 32 players), 3rd (4-1)
- R1: W 100-63 vs Genestealer Cults
- R2: W 85-84 vs T'au Empire
- R3: L 41-100 vs Death Guard
- R4: W 100-63 vs Necrons
- R5: W 90-72 vs Adeptus Custodes

The only loss was to Death Guard. On Halloween this same list beat Death Guard 94-54, a different player. I don't know what happened in the GT loss, so I'm not going to claim it's a bad matchup.

Before this run, Cook took a similar Knight Tyrant list with Be'lakor and a Keeper of Secrets to Hallowtide Redux (Oct 17, 35 players) and went 2-3. He then cut Be'lakor and the Keeper for two more Screamer units and two Plaguebearer blocks. Next two events with that version: 7-1.

That 4-1 was Daemons' only X-1 at qualifying GTs that weekend. Listhammer had Chaos Daemons at 45.7% over 105 games, 21 players, with no event wins and no undefeated lists. Most-brought was Blood Legion + Warptide (5 players, 38.5%). One weekend, small sample.

Bonus from the same weekend, a 3-round event: Luca Vella's Khorne list with Be'lakor went 3-0 and took 2nd of 47 at Warhammer 40k Modena (Italy, Nov 8). Blood Legion + Cavalcade of Chaos, 1,995 pts: Be'lakor (Warlord), Skarbrand, Rendmaster on Blood Throne, 12 Bloodcrushers with 3 Skullmasters, 2 units of Flesh Hounds, 2 Skull Cannons. Rounds: R1 W 86-71 vs Dark Angels, R2 W 67-55 vs Grey Knights, R3 W 76-44 vs Aeldari.

Lists are as published on Listhammer. Credit to J.R. Cook and Luca Vella. Points are still Munitorum Field Manual v1.6; nothing changed between the two Cook events.

Question for the thread: would you put a Knight Tyrant in your Daemons list? 390 points is a big cut. Is the gun platform and the big body worth it, or do you spend that elsewhere?

Full video if you want the detail: https://youtu.be/SVO0QxSZS9o
