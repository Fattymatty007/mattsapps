The World

The Blightlord's Boil
Foulfeast

A backwoods world once conquered by the Plague Marines, then given to Nurgle as a token of worship. Now it is all crumbling ruins, rocks that weep green oil, and pools of rotted mucus that birth daemons. Flies hang thick in the air. Storms almost never come here, so when the thunder starts, something is very wrong.

Characters

Daemon Prince of Nurgle
Malecore

Newly risen from the Boil, black and red, with crimson-membraned wings and a hellforged plague axe. Strangely, his body is untouched by the Plague Father's gifts. He has a lot to prove before he can be a general of Nurgle's army, and two very opinionated elders are watching him try.

The Tallyman of Nurgle
Epidemius

Nurgle's renowned Herald and record-keeper. Carried on a throne by giggling nurglings and guarded by plaguebearers, he judges and tallies everything he sees in his ledger, hourglass close at hand. He has his own idea of what a worthy trial looks like.

The Rain Father
Rotigus

A towering Greater Daemon of Nurgle, wielding the Gnarlrod. Nobody invited him to Malecore's trials. He came anyway, because he believes there is much to gain from the young prince's success. He and Epidemius are... not close.

Legendary Soulgrinder
The Desecrator

A colossal daemon engine of metal and warp-flesh, with one fleshy warp claw bristling with guns, one plated metal claw, and burning red eyes. It's the biggest thing on the battlefield, and it walks straight over anything in its way.
