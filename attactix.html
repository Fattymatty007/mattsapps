Learn it in 60 seconds

  1. Setup: 30 life each. Both players hold the same 8 cards.
  2. Each turn: secretly pick 1 card, then reveal together.
  3. Combat: attacks roll D6s. Swift Strike hits first. Block stops all damage. Parry steals a die and deals its face value back.
  4. Cycles: played cards stay face-up where both players can see them. When all 8 are spent, take them back — life carries over.
  5. Winning: drop your rival to 0 life. Both hit 0 on the same turn: draw.

The eight cards

Overhead SwingATTACK

Roll 2D6, discard the lowest, deal the rest. The reliable bruiser.

Swift StrikeATTACK

Roll 1D6 and deal it before everything else. Can end a rival before they heal.

Final AssaultATTACK

Roll 1D6 and triple it. Stack it with Power Charge for the telegraphed one-shot.

BlockTACTIC

Prevent all damage this turn. The only clean answer to a charged-up finisher.

RecoverTACTIC

Roll 2D6, gain that much life — even past 30. Greedy, until a Swift Strike lands first.

Power ChargeTACTIC

Bank a D6 in front of you. It rides your next attack — mandatory, even into a Block. They stack.

Copy CatTACTIC

Become whatever your rival played — timing included. Double Copy Cat: nothing happens.

ParryTACTIC

Steal one D6 from their attack roll, deal its face value back, remove it. Catches everything — even Swift Strike.

Why it works

ATTACTIX is a reading game, not a luck game. There are no hidden cards — every charge is banked in public, every discard sits face-up, so you always know exactly which answers your rival still holds. The famous "unfair" combo — Final Assault stacked with Power Charges — takes two dead turns to build, happens in plain sight, and dies to a single Block. If you walk into it, you earned it.

It's also a game with nothing to buy. One deck is the whole game: no boosters, no meta decks, no second edition invalidating your cards. Learn it in a minute, spend a year out-reading your friends.
